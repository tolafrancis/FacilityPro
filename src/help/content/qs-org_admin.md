You run FacilityPro for your organisation. In your first hour, set up the basics so your team can start reporting and fixing faults.

## Your first hour

1. **Check your organisation** — **Settings → General**: name, language, **timezone** and **currency**. Click **Save**. → [Settings](/help/guide/settings#general)
2. **Map your buildings** — **Locations → Add site**, then add buildings, floors, rooms and zones. → [Facilities](/help/guide/facilities)
3. **Add your key equipment** — **Assets → Add asset**, starting with the critical items (chillers, lifts, pumps, generators). → [Assets](/help/guide/assets#adding-an-asset)
4. **Review the catalogs** — **Settings → Catalogs**: fault types and their default priority, and asset types. **Load default types** gives you a starter list. → [Catalogs](/help/guide/settings#catalogs)
5. **Set response targets** — **Settings → SLA**. → [SLA targets](/help/guide/settings#sla-targets)
6. **Invite your team** — **Settings → Team & roles**: invite managers and technicians by email, and create a **join link** for tenants. → [Inviting people](/help/guide/user-management#inviting-people)
7. **Switch on automation** — **Workflows**: *Auto-create work orders*, and routing workflows so jobs go to the right technician. → [Workflows](/help/guide/settings#workflows-automation)
8. **Plan preventive maintenance** — **Maintenance → New schedule** for your critical assets. → [Preventive maintenance](/help/guide/preventive-maintenance)

## Every week

- Look at the **Dashboard** for overdue work, and at **Approvals**.
- Open **Reports**: PM on-time completion, average resolution time, low stock and expiring contracts.
- Check **Vendors** for contracts and licences **Expiring soon**.

> **Tip:** Switch on two-factor authentication under **Security**. Your account can change everything. → [Security](/help/guide/user-management#your-own-account-security)
