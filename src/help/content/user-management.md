Everyone who uses FacilityPro belongs to your organisation with a **role**. The role decides what they see and can do. Administrators manage members, invitations and join links.

**Who can use it:** Administrators (*Settings → Team & roles*). Everyone manages their own security under **Security**.

## Roles and what they can do

- **Administrator** — everything, including team and roles, settings, billing and deleting records.
- **Manager** — runs day-to-day maintenance: requests, work orders (including verifying and closing), maintenance plans, assets, parts, vendors, approvals, workflows, reports and financials. Cannot manage the team.
- **Technician** — works on assigned jobs (**My work**): starts, logs parts and labour, fills in checklists and resolves. Sees assets, parts, locations and devices. Cannot see reports, financials or settings.
- **Occupant** (tenant) — a simple app: report a fault, follow *My requests* and read announcements. Sees nothing else.
- **Vendor** — an outside contractor account. See the note below.

> **Note:** At the moment a **Vendor** account sees the same pages as a technician, including all work orders. Only give this role to contractors you trust with that information. See [Known issues](/help/known-issues).

## Team & roles

![Team & roles](/help/screens/settings-team.webp)

① Members · ② Change role · ③ Join links & QR codes · ④ Invite a member

Go to **Settings → Team & roles**.

- **Members** ① lists everyone, identified by email address.
- Change someone's role in the drop-down ②. It takes effect at once.
- The **bin** removes a person from the organisation. Their past work stays on record.

## Inviting people

1. Under **Invite a member** ④, type the person's email address.
2. Choose their role and click **Invite**.
3. They get an email with a link. You can also copy the link and send it yourself.
4. **Pending invites** shows who hasn't joined yet. Copy a link again, or revoke an invite with the bin.

## Join links and QR codes

A join link lets many people join with one link: ideal for tenants of a building, or a new shift of technicians.

1. Under **Join links & QR codes** ③, choose the role they **Join as**.
2. Optionally give it a **Name** (e.g. *Tower A tenants*), an **Expires** time and a **Max. people**.
3. Click **Create link**.
4. Click **Share** to copy the link, send it on WhatsApp or Zalo, or print its **QR code** for a noticeboard.
5. **Revoke link** stops it working.

> **Warning:** Anyone with a join link can join with that role. Never share a Manager or Administrator link publicly.

## Member profiles and site access

- **Profile** — for technicians: employee ID, phone, labour rate, shift, skills and certifications ([Technicians & teams](/help/guide/technicians-teams#technician-profiles)).
- **Site access** — tick the sites a person may work on. With nothing ticked, they see every site.

## Your own account security

![Security](/help/screens/security.webp)

1. Go to **Security**.
2. Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Enable 2FA**. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, then enter the **Verification code** and click **Verify & enable**.
3. From then on, sign-in asks for a code from the app.
4. Choose how you are notified (email, SMS, push). See [Notifications](/help/guide/notifications#choosing-email-sms-or-push).

> **Tip:** Administrators and managers should switch on 2FA.
