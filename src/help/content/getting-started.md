FacilityPro keeps everything about your buildings in one place: where things are, what equipment you have, what is broken, who is fixing it and what it cost. This page gets you signed in and shows you around.

> **Note:** The screenshots in this guide come from the demo organisation **Harbourview Properties (Demo)**. Every name, place and number in it is fictional. Your own screens show your own data.

## Signing in

![Sign in](/help/screens/signin.webp)

① Email address · ② Password · ③ Keep me logged in · ④ Forgot password? · ⑤ Sign in · ⑥ Create an account

1. Open FacilityPro and go to **Sign in**.
2. Type your **email address** ① and **password** ②.
3. Tick **Keep me logged in** ③ on your own device only.
4. Click **Sign in** ⑤.

> **Tip:** Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** ④ and follow the link we email you.

> **Warning:** Do not tick **Keep me logged in** on a shared or public computer.

## Creating an account

1. Click **Create an account** ⑥ on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your name, email, phone and a password.
3. Enter the verification code we send you (or click the link in the email).
4. If someone invited you, you join their organisation straight away. Otherwise you are asked to set up a new organisation.

## Setting up a new organisation

When you sign up without an invitation, a short setup runs:

1. Type your **organisation name** and choose your **industry**.
2. Optionally upload your **logo** (it replaces the "F" in the top-left corner).
3. Click **Let's start planning**. You land on the [Dashboard](/help/guide/dashboard) as the **Administrator**.

Next, follow the **To Do List** on the dashboard: invite your team, add a location, an asset and your spare parts. See [Dashboard → To-do list](/help/guide/dashboard#the-to-do-list).

## Finding your way around

![The dashboard with its main controls](/help/screens/dashboard.webp)

① Menu · ② Organisation and your role · ③ Create · ④ ? Help · ⑤ Notifications · ⑥ Language · ⑦ Key numbers

- **Menu ①** — every module, in groups: *Maintenance Operations*, *Operations & control*, *Financial* and *Intelligence & settings*. Click a group title to fold it.
- **Organisation and role ②** — which organisation you are in and your role (Administrator, Manager, Technician, Occupant or Vendor). Your role decides what you can see and do. See [User management → Roles](/help/guide/user-management#roles-and-what-they-can-do).
- **Create ③** — add almost anything from one place.
- **? Help ④** — explains the page you are on. See [Help on every page](#help-on-every-page).
- **Notifications ⑤** — see [Notifications](/help/guide/notifications).
- **Language ⑥** — switch between **EN** and **VI** at any time.

## Using FacilityPro on a phone

![FacilityPro on a phone](/help/screens/mobile-dashboard.webp)

On a phone, the menu moves to a bar at the bottom of the screen. The four most-used pages for your role are there, plus **Menu** for everything else. Forms, buttons and photo uploads are sized for touch. If you lose signal, changes are saved on the phone and sent when you are back online.

## Help on every page

- Click **? Help** in the top bar to see what the current page is for, what you can do there and what each field means.
- Click **User guide** in the menu to open this Help Center.
- Click **Take the tour** (in Help) for a one-minute walk-through.

![The guided tour](/help/screens/tour.webp)

① Step counter · ② Don't show this tour again · ③ Skip tour · ④ Previous · ⑤ Next / Finish

The tour starts by itself the first time you open the home screen. **Skip tour** ③ hides it until your next visit. Tick **Don't show this tour again** ② (or finish it) to stop it for good. You can restart it any time from **? Help → Take the tour**.
