**Locations** describe where everything is. Every asset, request and work order points to a location, so set these up first. The same section covers bookable **desks** and **facilities** (meeting rooms, gyms, terraces).

**Who can use it:** Administrators and Managers add and change locations. Everyone else can see them and pick them in forms.

**Where:** Menu → **Locations**, **Desks**, **Facilities**.

## How locations are organised

Locations form a tree with four levels:

1. **Site** — a property or campus, e.g. *Harbourview Tower*.
2. **Building** — e.g. *Tower A*.
3. **Floor** — e.g. *Level 12*.
4. **Room** or **Zone** — a room is enclosed (*Plant room 5A*); a zone is an open area (*Main lobby*, *Car park B1*).

## Adding a site, building, floor, room or zone

1. Go to **Locations**.
2. To start a new property, click **Add site** ①. Enter the name in English and, if you like, in Tiếng Việt, and an optional address. Click **Save**.
3. On the site's row, click **+ Add building** ②.
4. On the building's row, click **+ Add floor** ③.
5. On the floor's row, click **+ Add room** ④ or **+ Add zone** ⑤.

> **Tip:** You can also add a location from **Dashboard → Create → Location**. Choose its **Type** and what it is **Inside**.

## Renaming or deleting a location

- Click the **pencil** ⑥ to rename.
- Click the **bin** to delete. You are asked to confirm.

> **Warning:** Deleting cannot be undone. Move or delete the assets in a location before you delete it.

## Desks (hot-desking)

![Desks](/help/screens/desks.webp)

1. Go to **Desks** and click **New desk**. Give it a name and choose its **zone** (for example *Hot-desk zone 2B*).
2. To book, under **Make a booking** choose the desk, type the booker's name, pick the date and click **Book**.
3. **Recent bookings** lists what was booked.

## Facilities (meeting rooms, gym, BBQ area…)

![Facilities](/help/screens/facilities.webp)

1. Go to **Facilities** and click **New facility**. Enter the name, location and number of seats.
2. Book it the same way as a desk: choose the facility, the booker's name and the date, then click **Book**.

> **Tip:** A new booking can start an automation, for example emailing reception. See [Settings → Workflows](/help/guide/settings#workflows-automation).
