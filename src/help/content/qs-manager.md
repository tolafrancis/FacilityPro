You keep maintenance moving: new requests become assigned work, jobs get finished on time, and the right parts are in stock.

## Your daily routine

1. **Dashboard** — check the overdue banner, **Pending Tasks** and **Recent Activity**. → [Dashboard](/help/guide/dashboard)
2. **Requests** — open anything **New**. Click **Create work order**, or **Reject** if it isn't a maintenance job. → [Turning a request into a work order](/help/guide/work-orders#step-2-turning-a-request-into-a-work-order)
3. **Work orders** — filter by **Status: Open (unassigned)** and assign each job. → [Assigning the work](/help/guide/work-orders#step-3-assigning-the-work)
4. **Resolved work** — check it, then choose **→ Verified** and **→ Closed**. → [Verifying and closing](/help/guide/work-orders#step-6-verifying-and-closing-manager)
5. **Approvals** — approve or reject waiting requests. → [Approvals](/help/guide/work-orders#approvals-for-expensive-work)
6. **Devices** — look at critical IoT alerts and make sure someone has acknowledged them. → [IoT alerts](/help/guide/iot#alerts)

## Every week

- **Maintenance** — check for overdue schedules; click **Generate due now** if needed. → [Preventive maintenance](/help/guide/preventive-maintenance)
- **Parts** — restock anything showing **Low stock**, or raise a purchase order. → [Inventory](/help/guide/inventory)
- **Reports** — export work orders to CSV for your weekly meeting. → [Reports](/help/guide/reports)

> **Tip:** Put a **TV display** in the control room so the whole team sees live job status. → [TV displays](/help/guide/settings#tv-displays)
