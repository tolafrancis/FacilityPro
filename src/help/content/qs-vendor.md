You are a contractor working for the organisation, for example on lifts or chillers. You use FacilityPro to see and update the jobs you are responsible for.

## What you can do

1. **See your jobs** — open **Work orders** and filter or search for yours. The organisation shows which jobs are yours in the **Vendor** field. → [Finding work orders](/help/guide/work-orders#finding-work-orders)
2. **Print a job sheet** — on a job, click **Print job sheet** for the details and signature lines. → [Job sheets](/help/guide/work-orders#step-3-assigning-the-work)
3. **Report a fault** you find on site with **Create → Report a fault**.
4. **Check permits** — risky work (hot work, confined spaces) must have an **approved** permit first. → [Permits to work](/help/guide/vendors#permits-to-work)

> **Note:** The Vendor role currently sees the same pages as a technician. Moving a job's status is limited to the person it is assigned to and managers, so ask the manager to update jobs you are named on as vendor. See [Known issues](/help/known-issues).
