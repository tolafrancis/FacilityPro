**Reports** show how maintenance is going: open work, overdue jobs, response times, preventive-maintenance compliance, stock and spending. You can export the raw data and see who changed what.

**Who can use it:** Administrators and Managers.

**Where:** Menu → **Reports** and **Financial**.

## The reports page

![Reports](/help/screens/reports.webp)

① Filters · ② Operational metrics · ③ Cost & spend · ④ CSV exports · ⑤ Activity log

### Filters ①

Choose **From** and **To** dates, a **Location**, a **Technician** and a **Priority**. Every figure and chart below updates.

### Operational metrics ②

- **Open requests** and **Open work orders**.
- **Overdue work orders** — past their due date and not yet resolved.
- **Avg resolution (hrs)** — average time from creation to resolution.
- **PM due** — maintenance schedules due now.
- **PM on-time completion** — the share of preventive work finished by its due date.
- **Low-stock parts** — at or below their reorder level.
- **Contracts expiring (30d)**.

### Cost & spend ③

- **Total cost (filtered)** — labour, parts and recorded spending.
- **Of total budget** — spending as a share of your budgets.
- **Planned vs. reactive** — the share of cost that was planned (PM) work.
- **Mean time between failures** — the average number of days between breakdowns.
- **Cost by month (last 6 months)** and **Top vendor spend** bar charts.

## Exporting data

1. Set the filters.
2. Under **Exports** ④, click **Export requests (CSV)** or **Export work orders (CSV)**.
3. Open the file in Excel or Google Sheets.

## The activity log

The **Activity log** ⑤ lists the latest changes, such as requests created and work orders updated, with the time and type of change. Use it to answer "when did this change?".

## Financial overview

![Financial operations](/help/screens/financial.webp)

**Financial** shows **Budgeted**, **Committed spend** and **Open purchase orders** at the top. Below them you manage:

- **Procurement** — purchase orders ([Inventory → Purchase orders](/help/guide/inventory#purchase-orders)).
- **Customers** — tenants or clients you bill.
- **Payments** — money paid, linked to a vendor, purchase order or invoice.
- **Expenditures** — spending, linked to a work order, asset, vendor and cost center.
- **Schedule of rates** — standard prices, e.g. labour per hour.
- **Budget**, **Cost centers** and **Expense categories**.

> **Tip:** Link every expenditure to a cost center (e.g. *CC-100 — Harbourview Tower operations*). Budgets and reports can then be compared per building.

## TV display boards

A TV display shows live work-order status on a screen in the control room or lobby, without anyone signing in. Set it up under [Settings → TV displays](/help/guide/settings#tv-displays).
