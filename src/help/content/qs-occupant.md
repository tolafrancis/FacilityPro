You live or work in one of the buildings. Use FacilityPro to report anything broken and follow it until it's fixed.

![Tenant home](/help/screens/tenant-home.webp)

① Report a fault · ② My requests · ③ Announcements

## Reporting a fault

1. Tap **Report a fault** ①.
2. Write a short **Summary**, e.g. *Bathroom ceiling dripping*.
3. Choose the **Fault type**, how serious it is (**Severity**) and the **Location**.
4. Describe the problem and add a **Photo** if you can.
5. Tap **Submit report**.

→ With pictures: [Work orders → Step 1](/help/guide/work-orders#step-1-a-fault-is-reported)

## Following your request

1. Open **My requests** ②.
2. Each request shows its status: **New** → **Assigned** → **In progress** → **Resolved** → **Closed**. (**On hold** means waiting, for example for a part. **Rejected** means it isn't a maintenance job.)
3. The bell tells you when the status changes. → [Notifications](/help/guide/notifications)

## Announcements

Building news, such as planned water shut-offs, appears under **Announcements** ③ on your home page.

> **Tip:** See a QR code sticker on equipment? Scan it with your phone camera to report a fault on that exact item.
