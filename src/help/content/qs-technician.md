Your jobs are waiting in **My work**. This is everything you need to go from "assigned" to "resolved".

## Doing a job, step by step

1. Open **My work** (bottom bar on your phone). → [My work](/help/guide/technicians-teams#my-work-for-technicians)
2. Tap the job. Read the **Instructions**, **Location** and **Asset**.
3. When you start, change **Status** to **→ In progress**.
4. Take a **Before** photo under **Photo evidence**.
5. Work through the **Checklist** and tap **Save checklist**.
6. Log every part under **Parts used** (part + quantity → **Log part**).
7. Log your time under **Labor** (minutes → **Log labor**).
8. Take an **After** photo.
9. Under **Closing details**, choose the **Cause of failure** and **Completion code**. Enter the downtime.
10. Change **Status** to **→ Resolved**. Your manager verifies and closes it.

→ Full details: [Work orders → Step 4](/help/guide/work-orders#step-4-doing-the-work-technician)

## When you are stuck

- Waiting for a part? Choose **→ On hold** and type the reason. → [Putting work on hold](/help/guide/work-orders#putting-work-on-hold)
- Found another fault? Use **Create → Report a fault**, or **Report fault** on the asset's page.
- An IoT alert on your equipment? Open it under **Devices** and tap **Acknowledge**. → [IoT alerts](/help/guide/iot#alerts)

> **Tip:** No signal? Keep going. Your changes and photos are sent when you are back online.

> **Warning:** You cannot resolve a job until the completion code is set and the required checklist items are done.
