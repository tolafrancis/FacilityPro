FacilityPro tells people what they need to know: new jobs, status changes, approvals, low stock, expiring contracts and IoT alerts. It also lets you talk with tenants and send them announcements.

**Where:** the **bell** in the top bar, **Security** (your delivery choices), **Inbox**, **Tenant experience** and **Surveys**.

## The notification bell

![Notifications](/help/screens/notifications.webp)

① Mark all read · ② A notification

1. A red number on the **bell** shows how many notifications you haven't read.
2. Click the bell to open the list. The newest are at the top.
3. Click a notification ② to go to the page it is about (a work order, an approval, a device…).
4. Click **Mark all read** ① to clear the counter.

Typical notifications:

- **Assigned to you** — a work order is now yours (technicians).
- **Status changed** — a request you reported moved on (tenants).
- **Work order resolved** — ready for you to verify (managers).
- **Approval needed** — a work order is waiting for a decision.
- **Critical IoT alert** — a sensor crossed a threshold.
- **Low stock**, **contract expiring**, **preventive maintenance due**.

## Choosing email, SMS or push

![Security & notifications](/help/screens/security.webp)

1. Go to **Security** in the menu.
2. Under **Email notifications**, tick **Email me notifications** to receive them by email as well.
3. Under **Text messages (SMS)**, tick **Text me notifications** and enter your **Mobile number** with the country code (e.g. +84…).
4. Under **Push notifications**, click **Enable push on this device** and allow notifications when your browser or phone asks.

> **Note:** Email, SMS and push are sent by your FacilityPro provider's delivery service. If nothing arrives, ask your administrator to check **Settings → Integrations**.

## Inbox: conversations with tenants

![Inbox](/help/screens/inbox.webp)

The **Inbox** gathers messages from tenants and contacts on **Zalo**, **WhatsApp**, **LINE**, **email** and the **web**.

1. Click a conversation on the left to read it.
2. Type your reply and click **Send**. Each reply shows *sending…*, *sent*, *delivered* or *Not delivered*.
3. Click **Create work order** to turn the message into a job straight away.
4. **New conversation** starts one yourself (for example after a phone call).

> **Warning:** Replies on Zalo or WhatsApp are only delivered once your organisation's account is connected. See [Settings → Integrations](/help/guide/settings#integrations).

## Announcements to tenants

![Tenant experience](/help/screens/tenant-experience.webp)

1. Go to **Tenant experience**.
2. Under **Create a broadcast**, enter the **Title**, **Audience** (e.g. *All tenants*) and **Message**.
3. Click **Publish update**. It appears under **Announcements** on every tenant's home page.

> **Note:** Items marked **Draft** are not shown to tenants.

## Surveys

![Surveys](/help/screens/surveys.webp)

Use **Surveys** to ask tenants how you are doing, for example after each job. Create a survey with its questions and mark it **active**. A workflow can send it automatically when a request is completed ([Workflows](/help/guide/settings#workflows-automation)).
