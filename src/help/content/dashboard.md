The dashboard is the first page staff see after signing in. It shows how much work there is, what is overdue and what happened recently. Tenants (occupants) see a simpler home page instead — see [Quick start: Occupant](/help/quick-start/occupant).

**Who sees it:** Administrators, Managers, Technicians and Vendors.

## The top of the dashboard

![Dashboard](/help/screens/dashboard.webp)

⑦ **Key numbers:**

- **No. of Tasks** — all work orders.
- **Pending Tasks** — work orders not yet resolved.
- **No. of Assets** — assets in service.
- **Recently Completed Tasks** — work finished in the last 30 days.

If any work is past its due date, a red banner such as **"1 work order is overdue"** appears above the numbers. Open [Work orders](/help/guide/work-orders#finding-work-orders) and filter to find it.

The **Search requests** box finds requests by title or description.

## Charts and lists

![Dashboard charts](/help/screens/dashboard-charts.webp)

① **Weekly** — faults reported (dark bars) and resolved (light bars) for each of the last 7 days, with totals on the left.
② **Recent Activity** — the latest requests and their status. Click one to open it, or **View all**.
③ **Category** — faults by fault type over the last 30 days.
④ **To Do List** — setup steps for a new organisation (managers and administrators).

## The Create menu

![The Create menu](/help/screens/dashboard-create.webp)

Click **Create** to add:

- **Report a fault** — opens the fault form ([Work orders → Requests](/help/guide/work-orders#step-1-a-fault-is-reported)).
- **Asset**, **Part / spare**, **Location**, **Vendor** — a quick form asking only for the name and the most important field. Add more details later on the item's own page.
- **Maintenance plan**, **Checklist** — opens the relevant page.
- **Invite people** (Administrators).

> **Tip:** After a quick add, click **Add another** to enter several items in a row.

## The To-do list

For a new organisation, the **To Do List** shows four setup steps: **Team**, **Location**, **Asset** and **Parts Inventory**. Each has a **+** button that opens the quick form. A step is crossed out once it is done. **Invite** opens team invitations ([User management](/help/guide/user-management#inviting-people)).

> **Note:** Technicians and vendors see **Quick actions** instead of the To Do List.
