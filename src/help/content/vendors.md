**Vendors** are the outside companies you work with: contractors, suppliers and service providers. This section also tracks their **contracts**, your **licences and certificates**, **permits to work** and your **document** library.

**Who can use it:** Administrators and Managers add and edit. Technicians can view.

**Where:** Menu → **Vendors**, **Permits to work**, **Documents**.

## Vendors, contracts and licences

![Vendors & contracts](/help/screens/vendors.webp)

① New vendor · ② New contract · ③ New licence · ④ Expiring soon · ⑤ Expired

### Adding a vendor

1. Click **New vendor** ①.
2. Enter the **Name**, **Category** (e.g. *HVAC contractor*), **Email** and **Phone**.
3. Save. The vendor can now be chosen on work orders, purchase orders, parts (as the preferred supplier) and expenses.

### Adding a contract

1. Click **New contract** ②.
2. Enter the **Title**, choose the **Vendor**, add a **Document URL** (a link to the signed contract), the **Expiry date** and how many days before expiry to be reminded.
3. Save. Contracts close to expiry show **Expiring soon** ④. The **Contracts expiring (30d)** figure in [Reports](/help/guide/reports) counts them.

### Licences and certificates

Use **New licence** ③ for anything with an expiry date that your organisation holds, such as a fire safety certificate or a lift operating permit. Enter the **Holder** and **Expiry date**. Expired ones show **Expired** ⑤.

> **Tip:** Assign a vendor on a work order to see their active contracts right there. See [Work orders → Step 3](/help/guide/work-orders#step-3-assigning-the-work).

## Permits to work

![Permits to work](/help/screens/permits.webp)

A permit makes sure risky work (hot work, confined spaces, working at height, electrical isolation) is approved before it starts.

1. Go to **Permits to work**.
2. Under **Create permit**, enter the **Permit title**, **Asset or area**, **Requester** (often the contractor), **Approver**, **Due date** and **Notes** describing the hazards and controls.
3. Choose the **Status**: *Draft*, *Submitted*, *Approved* or *Rejected*.
4. Click **Submit permit**. Permits appear on the right with their status.

> **Warning:** Only start the work once the permit shows **approved**.

## Documents

![Document repository](/help/screens/documents.webp)

Keep manuals, SOPs, certificates and reports in one place.

1. Go to **Documents** → **Add a document**.
2. Enter the **Title**, **Category**, **Owner** and **Summary**.
3. Add a **Link or reference** (https://…) or **Upload file**.
4. Under **Link to a record**, choose the asset, vendor or work order it belongs to. It then shows on that record's page.
5. Choose **Who can see it**: *Staff only* or *Everyone* (including tenants).
6. Click **Save document**.
